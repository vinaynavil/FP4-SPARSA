Sparsity Engine

It only computes
what's not zero.

Every PE holds 4 parallel MAC lanes. Flip sparse_en below and watch the array gate off any lane whose operand decoded to zero — in silicon, that's a DSP48E1 slice multiplying zero by zero instead of a real value, cutting switching activity at the multiplier input.

CTRL.sparse_en
0.0% lanes gated
16 PEs × 4 lanes = 64 multiply lanes, each mapped 1:1 to a DSP48E1 (80 DSP48E1 used on-device in total, including one accumulate-stage DSP per PE).

63% is the measured figure on ResNet-20 / CIFAR-10: 25.3% weight sparsity and 50.5% activation sparsity combine, since a MAC lane is gated whenever either operand is zero. Gating affects switching power only — it does not change cycle count or throughput.

Architecture

A systolic array,
refined.

Weights stay put. Activations stream through. Decode and raw zero checking happen cleanly with 4-stage pipelining.

  • Pre-decode & zero detection at array boundaryActivations and weights convert to 8-bit operands in systolic_array.v before reaching PEs.
  • 4-lane MAC per PE64 DSP48E1 multiply lanes plus 1 accumulate-stage DSP48E1 per PE — 80 DSP48E1 total, MREG and AREG both pinned for 350.017 MHz closure.
  • 18-bit saturating accumulatorOne guard bit, no silent overflow, clipping to maximum values.

Verification

Checked, not just
synthesized.

A design that closes timing isn't the same as one that's verified. Both matter to the people who'll review this.

  • Self-checking Verilog testbenchAn inline golden reference model computes expected results in software and checks every test round automatically — no hand-typed expected values.
  • FP4 E2M1 decoder verificationAll 16 codes checked against their expected decoded values, including FTZ behavior on subnormals.
  • Sparse vs. dense mode comparisonsparse_en toggled across test vectors to confirm functional equivalence with operand isolation on and off — gating changes power, not correctness.
  • 22 / 22 regression tests passedFull suite, including AXI-Lite register access, weight BRAM read/write, ping-pong buffering, subnormal FTZ, multi-vector streaming, and randomized stimulus.
  • Timing closure achievedWNS +0.205 ns (WHS +0.082 ns) at 350.017 MHz on the target Kintex-7 (xc7k160tfbg676-2) — post-implementation, not just post-synthesis.
